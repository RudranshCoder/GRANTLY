# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Students looking for scholarships and managing their applications.

## Product Purpose

Grantly helps students discover scholarships and track application progress.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The app supports student profiles, scholarship browsing and matching, saved scholarships, application tracking, and deadline reminders. Scholarship information is maintained in the project data; no external verification service is represented in the current app.

## Brand Commitments

The product is named Grantly. Its current mark is `public/grantly-mark.svg`.

## Evidence on Hand

Scholarship records and app behavior are present in `src/data/scholarships.ts` and the dashboard implementation. The current scholarship data is project-provided; do not imply independent verification beyond its recorded status.
